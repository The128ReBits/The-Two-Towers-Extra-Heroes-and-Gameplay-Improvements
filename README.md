## My additions — The128ReBits

- **Six playable adaptations:** Elven warrior, Elven archer, Cave troll, Rohan captain, Uruk captain and Gandalf the White. The roster grows from 11 to 17, using the original game’s models and animations.
- **Combat revisions across all 13 extras**, including the original port’s seven: native moves, stance, movement, transitions, weapons, reach and damage events. The troll keeps its size, heavy movement, mace and rocks. Xbox animation timing/sign/interpolation was corrected.
- **Campaign progression and individual records:** separate XP, purchases and scores; four extra-hero modes, including unlocked with real progression. New installs default to that mode; updates preserve your choice. Test mode remains without save writes.
- **Official voice-bank activation** for English, Spanish, Italian, French and German. The recordings belong to the original game’s rights holders.
- **Five independent language settings:** text, subtitles, gameplay voices, in-engine cutscene voices and movies. English fallback remains; official PAL text is labelled separately from community translations.
- **Optional media supplements:** Xbox PAL for specific additional voices, official texts and localized movies; Spanish/Italian/French/German PS2 ISOs for verified gameplay voice events. The public installer does not import PS2 movies.
- **My GLOS69 Ultra/HD textures, Native and Lite**, downloaded separately: [Native](https://www.moddb.com/mods/the-two-towers-textures-4k-ai-remaster/addons/the-two-towers-textures-4k-ai-remaster-575gb-version-native) · [Lite](https://www.moddb.com/mods/the-two-towers-textures-4k-ai-remaster/addons/the-two-towers-textures-4k-ai-remaster-lite-version-175gb). Import a matching RAR or extracted folder. Textures work without DLSS.
- **Optional experimental DLSS 5 setup:** verified downloads and activation, customization/profiles, my menu/movie guard, TT_Acabado, TT_Sombra and Tierra Media – Sombra. External technologies keep their authors and licences; successful installation alone does not prove active GPU processing.
- **Installer/configurator and maintenance:** existing game or original port ZIP/folder plus your Xbox USA ISO; separate component steps, five interface languages, repair/uninstall/recovery, separate mod saves, preserved settings/profiles, backups, SHA-256 manifests and rollback.
- Tests, media auditing and documentation. Automated decoding does not prove spoken language or certify a complete playthrough.

## Install R8

1. Download and extract the **complete release ZIP**.
2. Run **Instalar The Two Towers Definitive Mod.exe**.
3. Choose an existing port/game installation, or a new destination with the original Windows port ZIP/folder and your own recognized Xbox USA ISO.
4. Choose languages, optional PAL/PS2 voice supplements, HD texture import and optional DLSS in their dedicated steps.
5. Review the summary, install and open the configurator. Back up your saves and retain your original port.

**Required:** the original Windows port and your legally owned Xbox USA game. **PAL alone is not a supported standalone executable base.** No game data, ISO, saves, GLOS69 texture pack, film-still shop portraits or keys are bundled. Source release naming remains Definitive Mod R8 inside the player ZIP.
