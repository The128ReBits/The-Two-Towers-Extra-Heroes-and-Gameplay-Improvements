# The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers — Extra Heroes & Gameplay Improvements

**Gameplay additions by The128ReBits for [Gumpolas’ original The Two Towers Recompiled PC port](https://github.com/Gumpolas/TooTawersRecomp).**

Take the Cave troll into battle with its huge frame, heavy movement, mace and throwable rocks. Add six playable characters, revisit combat across all 13 extra heroes and choose text, subtitles and voices independently. The roster grows from **11 to 17**.

## Included gameplay and language work

- Six additions: **Elven warrior, Elven archer, Cave troll, Rohan captain, Uruk captain, Gandalf the White**. Original game models/animations; roster 11 → 17.
- **Playable Cave troll:** original size and heavy movement, native attacks adapted to player controls, mace and rocks; targeted contact, close-target, reach and rock-launch revisions. Individual records are integrated.
- Combat revisions for **13 extras**, including Gumpolas’ seven. Native combo fragments, stance, movement, transitions, weapons and damage events; Xbox animation codec correction. Examples: Frodo’s timing/sign handling and weapon profile, Lurtz’s multi-hit segmentation, native captain attacks and distinct Gandalf the White presentation.
- Campaign progression with extras; individual XP, purchases and scores. Four modes: off, normal unlocking, tests without saves, all available with real progression. New installs choose the last; updates retain your choice.
- Five independent settings: text, subtitles, gameplay voices, in-engine cutscene voices, videos. Official English/Spanish/Italian/French/German voice-bank activation, optional PAL/PS2 supplementation, English fallback. Official PAL texts are labelled separately from community translations.
