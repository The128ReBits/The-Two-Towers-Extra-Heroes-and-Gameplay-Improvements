## HD texture/DLSS installer & gameplay improvements for Gumpolas’ PC port

**By The128ReBits. Original port by @Gumpolas and contributors.** Special thanks for the port, its continuing improvements, native support for my GLOS69 textures and the GitHub link that gave them more visibility.

R8 includes six additional heroes (17 roster entries), revised combat across 13 extras, troll mace/rocks, animation fixes, individual records and campaign progression, five independent language choices, optional PAL/PS2 voice supplements, separate GLOS69 Native/Lite import, and optional experimental DLSS 5 download/activation/customization. Installer: existing game or original port ZIP/folder + your Xbox USA ISO; separate components and maintenance, save isolation, preserved settings/profiles and SHA-256/rollback.

### Download and install

Extract the **complete ZIP**, then run **Instalar The Two Towers Definitive Mod.exe**. Bring [Gumpolas’ original Windows port](https://github.com/Gumpolas/TooTawersRecomp/releases) and your own compatible Xbox USA game. HD textures are separate downloads. PAL alone is not a standalone base; the public installer imports PS2 gameplay voice supplements, not PS2 videos.
