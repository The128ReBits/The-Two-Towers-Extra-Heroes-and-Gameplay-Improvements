THE128REBITS — AUTHOR OF THE ULTRA/HD TEXTURE PACK AND THESE IMPROVEMENTS

The GLOS69 Ultra/HD texture pack, in Native and Lite versions, is authored by The128ReBits, as are the additions and integrations detailed here. Original game dubs, models, animations and other assets retain their rights holders; their activation and integration are contributions of this mod.

  • ULTRA/HD TEXTURES: Ultra/HD texture pack GLOS69 Native/Lite — created by The128ReBits, separate ModDB download
  • LANGUAGES, VOICES AND VIDEOS: activation of the five official spoken languages in gameplay and cutscenes; five independent language settings; official PAL text integration and PAL videos; disc-base detection
  • DLSS-5 AND PROFILES: DLSS-5 integration based on the author’s Simpsons Hit & Run pack: tt_dlss5_guard, TT_Acabado, TT_Sombra, Tierra Media – Sombra and the profile configurator.
  • SIX NEW CHARACTERS: Six new extras: Elven warrior, Elven archer, Cave troll, Rohan captain, Uruk captain and Gandalf the White.
  • COMBAT, PROGRESSION AND SYSTEM: Combat rework of all 13 extras, troll mace and rocks, corrected Xbox animation codec, unlocks and progression, separate saves, installer, tests and documentation.
  • OPTIONAL PS2 SUPPLEMENT AND COVERAGE: Optional: choose the PS2 ISO for each selected spoken language. Added gameplay cues over NTSC: ES +8, IT +5, FR +6, DE +7; after Xbox PAL: +4, +2, +4, +4. English covers what is still missing. See “Compare voices and ISOs”. No PS2 video import; listening/gameplay checks are pending.
